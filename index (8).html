Frequently Asked Questions

Can you provide GST invoicing for corporate bookings?

Yes — we issue full GST invoices and can work with your procurement team on PO-based bookings.

Do you have experience with large IT campus events?

Yes. Our DJs regularly perform at Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, and other major tech campus events with 500+ attendees.

Can the DJ also act as emcee?

We can provide a separate professional emcee, or our DJ can handle light hosting (announcements, awards intro). For full hosting, we recommend a dedicated MC.

What about background music during cocktails and dinner?

Standard. We bring lounge/jazz/instrumental sets for cocktails and dinner, then transition to party music after.
