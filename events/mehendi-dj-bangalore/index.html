Frequently Asked Questions

Is mehendi music different from sangeet music?

Yes — mehendi calls for softer, lower-tempo music since guests are seated and getting henna applied. Sangeet is high-energy with dancing.

Can you bundle mehendi + sangeet + wedding?

Yes — bundled multi-event packages save 15–25% versus individual bookings.

Do you provide outdoor sound setup?

Yes — outdoor mehendi setups (lawns, terraces, farmhouses) are standard. We bring weather-appropriate equipment.

Can guests request songs?

Of course. Our DJs welcome live requests and will balance them with the curated set.
