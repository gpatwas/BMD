Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do kids' birthday parties?

Yes — we have DJs experienced with kids' parties, including game music, cartoon themes, and interactive crowd play.

Can you do a small apartment terrace party?

Yes — we offer compact setups designed for apartment rooftops, balconies, and small venues without disturbing neighbors.

How long is a typical birthday DJ booking?

Most birthday parties book 2–4 hours. Extensions are charged hourly and easy to add on the day.

Yes — wireless mic provided for announcements, song dedications, and cake-cutting. Games can be coordinated too.
