How to Choose the Best DJ in Bangalore: A Practical Checklist

By the BookMyDJs team · Updated 31 October 2026 · 7 min read

Bangalore has hundreds of DJs, from wedding specialists in Jayanagar to club regulars in Koramangala and Indiranagar. The difference between a good night and a flat one usually comes down to a few decisions made weeks before the event. This guide walks you through them in order: what to decide first, what to check, what to ask, and what to get in writing.

Start with your event, not the DJ

Before you compare anyone, write down five things. Every DJ will ask for them, and they decide which DJs are even a fit:

  • Event type: wedding function, sangeet, reception, corporate event, birthday or house party.
  • Guest count and age mix: 40 friends in an apartment needs a very different setup from 400 guests at a banquet hall.
  • Venue: indoor or outdoor, the hall's power supply, and any sound restrictions.
  • Hours: when music starts and ends, including breaks for dinner, speeches or rituals.
  • Music languages: Bangalore crowds often want Bollywood, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi and English hits in one night.

1. Pick a DJ who specialises in your kind of event

A DJ who is brilliant in a club may struggle at a wedding, where they have to cue the bride's entry, play soft music during dinner and then fill the dance floor for three generations. Ask how many events like yours they did in the last year, and ask for videos from those events, not only club sets.

  • Weddings and receptions: look for experience with timed moments (entries, first dance, cake cutting) and a mic for the compère. See our wedding DJs and reception DJs.
  • Sangeet: the DJ must play choreographed tracks on cue and edit mashups to your dance timings. See sangeet DJs.
  • Corporate events: they need clean edits, a professional manner and experience with stage shows and award segments. See corporate event DJs.
  • House parties and birthdays: compact gear, and a DJ who respects apartment rules. See house party DJs and birthday DJs.

2. Set a realistic budget

DJ prices in Bangalore depend on hours, guest count, sound and lighting, and the date. Saturdays in wedding season (November to February) book out first and cost the most. These are the things that move the price up or down:

3. Check the music, not only the reputation

Ask for a 20 to 30 minute recorded mix and a sample playlist for an event like yours. Listen for smooth transitions, sensible volume and whether the song choices suit your guests. A good DJ:

  • asks for your must-play and do-not-play lists, ideally two weeks before the event;
  • takes guest requests without killing the energy on the dance floor;
  • can switch between Bollywood, regional hits, Punjabi and international chartbusters as the crowd changes;
  • plans quieter music for dinner and speeches, then builds the energy back up.

4. Make sure the equipment matches your venue

Sound that works in a 40-person living room will disappear in a 400-person banquet hall, and a hall-sized system will be painful in an apartment. Tell the DJ your guest count and venue size, and ask exactly what they will bring:

  • speakers and subwoofers sized for your guest count and room;
  • at least one wireless mic, and a second one for weddings and corporate events;
  • basic dance-floor lighting, with LED walls, fog or cold-pyro effects priced separately;
  • backup equipment: a spare laptop or media player, cables and a mic;
  • how much power the setup needs. Confirm this with the venue, especially for outdoor events.

5. Plan for Bangalore's practical rules

  • The 10 pm rule for outdoor sound: India's noise rules don't allow loudspeakers in open areas between 10 pm and 6 am. Lawns, terraces and open-air venues usually have to stop amplified music by 10 pm. If you want to dance later, choose an indoor hall.
  • Apartment and gated-community rules: many associations set their own sound limits and closing times for clubhouses and terraces. Check them before you book, and pick a DJ who brings a setup that suits apartments.
  • Traffic and setup time: a DJ usually needs 1.5 to 3 hours to set up and sound-check. Bangalore traffic can double travel time, so agree on an arrival time and confirm when the venue opens for vendors.
  • Venue tie-ups: some hotels and banquet halls have in-house or approved DJs, or charge an outside-vendor fee. Ask the venue before you pay an advance to anyone.

Choosing a DJ who knows your area helps with all of the above. See local DJs in Koramangala, Indiranagar, Whitefield, HSR Layout and Jayanagar.

6. Questions to ask before you book

  1. Are you available on my date, and will you perform yourself or send someone from your team?
  2. How many events like mine have you played in the last year? Can I see videos?
  3. What exactly is included in the price: hours, speakers, lights, mics, travel?
  4. What is your overtime rate per hour?
  5. What backup do you carry if a laptop, mixer or speaker fails?
  6. How do you handle song requests and our do-not-play list?
  7. How much advance do you need, and what is your cancellation policy?
  8. Can you coordinate with our event planner, anchor or choreographer?

7. Get it in writing

A short written agreement, even over email or WhatsApp, prevents most disputes. It should list the date, venue, start and end times, the equipment included, the total price, the advance paid, the overtime rate, travel charges and the cancellation terms. For weddings, add a simple run-sheet with entries, speeches and key songs, and share it with your planner.

Red flags to watch for

  • No videos from real events, only studio mixes or stock photos.
  • A quote with no breakdown of hours or equipment.
  • Pressure to pay a large advance immediately, or payment only to a personal account with no receipt.
  • Vague answers about who will actually turn up on the day.
  • No backup plan for equipment failure or a power cut.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book a DJ in Bangalore?

For weddings and weekend dates in peak season (November to February), book 2 to 3 months ahead. For other dates, 3 to 6 weeks is usually enough, and last-minute house parties can sometimes be arranged within 24 to 48 hours.

How much does a DJ cost in Bangalore?

There's no single rate. The price depends on the event type, the number of hours, guest count, sound and lighting, and the date. A small house party costs far less than a multi-function wedding or a large corporate show. Share your event details to get free, like-for-like quotes.

Can I play music outdoors after 10 pm in Bangalore?

Generally no. Loudspeakers aren't allowed in open areas between 10 pm and 6 am, so outdoor music usually has to stop by 10 pm. If your celebration will go later, plan to move indoors or choose an indoor venue.

Does the DJ bring the sound system and lights?

Most DJ packages include speakers, a mixer, a mic and basic lighting. LED walls, fog machines, cold pyro and extra speakers are usually add-ons, so ask for them to be listed in the quote.

Can one DJ cover the sangeet, mehendi and reception?

Yes. Many wedding DJs offer multi-function packages, and keeping the same DJ means they already know your family, your song lists and your venue. Confirm the hours for each function and whether the setup moves between venues.

The short version

Define your event first, shortlist DJs who specialise in it, compare like-for-like quotes, check real event videos, plan around Bangalore's sound rules and traffic, and put everything in writing.
