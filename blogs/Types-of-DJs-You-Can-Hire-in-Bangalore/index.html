Types of DJs You Can Hire in Bangalore

By the BookMyDJs team · Updated 13 November 2026 · 6 min read

Not every DJ is right for every event. The DJ who keeps a Koramangala club packed at midnight may not be the right person to cue the bride's entry, and a great wedding DJ may not be the best fit for a product launch. Knowing the main types helps you shortlist faster and ask the right questions. Here's a breakdown of the DJs you can hire in Bangalore and what each one is best at.

1. Wedding DJs

Wedding DJs specialise in long, multi-part celebrations. They know the flow of an Indian wedding: soft music during the mehendi, high energy for the baraat, cues for entries and the first dance, and a smooth move from cocktails to dinner to dancing at the reception. They're comfortable with a microphone and used to working with planners, anchors and photographers. Most can cover several functions across one or more days. See our wedding DJs and reception DJs.

2. Sangeet specialists

The sangeet has its own demands. Families rehearse dances for weeks, so the DJ must play the exact edited version of each track, in order, and start it on cue. Sangeet specialists prepare mashups to your choreography timings, keep a cue sheet, and often help at the rehearsal. Once the performances end, they switch to Bollywood and bhangra sets that bring every generation to the floor. See sangeet DJs.

3. Club and bar DJs

Club DJs are masters of the dance floor. They mix continuously, follow current hits closely, and can hold a crowd's energy for hours with EDM, house, hip-hop, techno or commercial sets. They're a great choice for after-parties, cocktail nights and venues with a nightlife feel. For family events, check that they're comfortable with Bollywood and older songs too, and with keeping volume at a level where guests can still talk early in the evening.

4. Corporate event DJs

Corporate DJs balance a professional setting with a good time. They play clean versions of songs, keep background music at a level that allows networking, and handle stage moments such as walk-in music for award winners, product reveals and speeches. Once the formal part is over, they switch to a full party set. Punctuality, appearance and clear communication with your event team matter as much as the music. See corporate event DJs.

5. Party DJs for birthdays, anniversaries and house parties

Party DJs are all-rounders for private celebrations. A child's birthday, a 30th with throwback hits and a 60th with classic favourites all need different music, and a good party DJ adapts to the age group and the theme. For apartments and villas, they bring compact setups and respect society rules on volume and timing. See birthday party DJs, anniversary DJs and house party DJs.

6. Mobile DJs and walk-in DJs

This is about equipment rather than style, and it's one of the first questions to settle:

  • Mobile DJs bring their own speakers, mixer, mics and lights. They're the right choice for homes, lawns, farmhouses, resorts and any venue without a sound system.
  • Walk-in DJs play on the venue's existing sound system. This works well at clubs, pubs and banquet halls with good in-house equipment. Check the venue's setup with the DJ in advance.

Our booking form asks which you prefer, so the quotes you receive match your venue.

Popular add-ons

  • Dhol with the DJ: a live dhol player alongside the DJ lifts a baraat, sangeet or mehendi.
  • Live musicians: a percussionist or saxophonist playing over the DJ's set adds a live feel to receptions and corporate parties.
  • Karaoke: great for family gatherings and office parties where guests like to sing.
  • Special effects: LED walls, fog, cold pyro and dance-floor lighting for big entries and first dances, usually priced separately.

How to pick the right type

  • Start with the event, not the DJ. A wedding needs a wedding DJ even if a club DJ has more followers.
  • Check your venue's sound system. No system means you need a mobile DJ.
  • Think about your guests. Mixed ages and languages call for a versatile DJ; a young crowd at a late-night party suits a club DJ.
  • Ask for videos of events like yours, not just studio mixes.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a mobile DJ and a walk-in DJ?

A mobile DJ brings their own sound system, mics and lights. A walk-in DJ uses the venue's equipment. If your venue has no sound system, or it's a home, lawn or farmhouse, you need a mobile DJ.

Can a club DJ play at a wedding?

Some can, but weddings need extra skills: timed entries, soft music for daytime functions, Bollywood and regional classics, and comfort with a microphone. Ask for videos from weddings before you book.

Do I need a separate DJ for the sangeet?

Not necessarily. Many wedding DJs handle the sangeet well. What matters is that they play your choreographed tracks exactly, on cue, so share your performance list and timings early.

Can one DJ handle both a corporate event and the after-party?

Yes. Many corporate DJs plan exactly that: professional background music and stage cues during the formal part, then a full dance set afterwards.

The short version

Match the DJ to your event: wedding and sangeet specialists for weddings, corporate DJs for company events, party DJs for birthdays and house parties, and club DJs for late-night dance floors.
